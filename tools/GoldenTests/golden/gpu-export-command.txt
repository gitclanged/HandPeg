refused:
  Copy: a Copy export keeps the file's own picture
  GIF: GIF and WebP are made by FFmpeg's palette steps
  deinterlaced: deinterlacing is FFmpeg's (the compositor has no deinterlacer yet)
  tone-mapped: tone mapping to SDR is FFmpeg's (the compositor has none yet)
  burned subtitles on a spliced main video: the source's subtitles are burned into a main video in several clips, on its own frames
  no known length: the main video's length is not known, so FFmpeg's canvas takes its clock from it

the sound rendered by the engine:
  ffmpeg -hide_banner -y -i D:/media/main.mp4 -f rawvideo -pix_fmt nv12 -video_size 320x180 -framerate 30/1 -i pipe:0 -f f32le -ar 48000 -ch_layout stereo -i D:/session/sound.f32 -map 1:v:0 -map 2:a:0
  as it stands: the engine's
  normalized: loudness normalization is FFmpeg's (it holds seconds back, so editing never hears it)
  no sound: the export has no sound track
  several tracks kept: the sound is kept as several tracks, and the sound engine renders one mix
  a copied track: a track is copied as it is, which only FFmpeg can do
