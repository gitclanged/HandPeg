# A Copy export that cannot cut is refused before it starts, with the reason (EncoderCommand.WhyCopyCannotCut).
a picture alone: Copy cannot export this Sequence: no clip on the timeline shows a file's picture, so there is nothing to copy. Choose a video codec on the Export tab to export it.
ok: a Sequence with no clip of a file's picture (a picture alone) is refused: there is nothing to copy
the file at other speeds: Copy cannot export this Sequence: the clips of match.mkv play at another speed, which a stream copy cannot cut without encoding. Choose a video codec on the Export tab to export it.
ok: a Sequence whose file's clips all play at another speed is refused: a stream copy cannot cut them
ok: an empty Sequence is refused as having nothing to copy
ok: a file cut into clips at speed 1 is not refused: it is copied through its cuts list
ok: a file played whole is not refused: it is copied as it is
