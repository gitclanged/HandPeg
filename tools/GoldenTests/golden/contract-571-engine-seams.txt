ok: the burst sits in the free control space (324..415, between CacheBudgetCap and the slot timings), aligned, overlapping nothing; the block is still 4096 bytes and protocol 9
ok: a post writes the source, hash, size and the finite times in order (NaN and negative ones dropped) under an odd version, then publishes the next even one
ok: the worker reads the whole burst under its even version

ok: an odd version (a post being written) is never read; a post after an interrupted one writes under the next odd number and publishes 12
