  RX 7900 XTX: 11 GB, 1 GB, 4 GB, 8 GB, 11 GB
  Adreno: 2 GB, 1 GB, 2 GB, 2 GB, 2 GB
  a maximum below the baseline holds the budget there from the start: 2 GB baseline, 1 GB maximum -> 1 GB
a step of growth is allowed (two seconds apart, below the maximum):
  the first step: yes
  a step 1.9 s after the last: no
  a step 2 s after the last: yes
  at the maximum (2 GB + 9 steps of an 11 GB ceiling): no
  one short of it: yes
letting go of a frame shows the cache too small (thrash) when:
  decoded 1 s ago, far from every moment of interest: thrash
  decoded 3.9 s ago: thrash
  decoded 4 s ago, no moment of interest: not
  decoded a minute ago, 1.5 s from the moment shown: thrash
  decoded a minute ago, 2 s from a hint: thrash
  decoded a minute ago, 2.5 s from the playhead: not
the free list keeps at most an eighth of the budget, at most 256 MB:
  a budget of 512 MB: 64 MB
  a budget of 2 GB: 256 MB
  a budget of 11 GB: 256 MB
